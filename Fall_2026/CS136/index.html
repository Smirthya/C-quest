Step 1/3 — Consent

Research Consent Form

Study Title: Enhancing Learning Through LLM-Powered Educational Games

Principal Investigator: Dr. Ashish Amresh · Northern Arizona University, SICCS

You are being asked to participate in a research study. Your participation is voluntary. This form contains important information about this study. Please read it carefully and feel free to ask questions before deciding to participate.

Why is this study being done?
This study examines the quality and effectiveness of AI-generated educational content in interactive learning environments, specifically for game-based learning. The research helps educators understand how to integrate AI-generated content into educational technology while maintaining quality standards.

What will happen if I take part in this study?
If you decide to participate, you will:

• Complete a C language quiz and game session covering 8 topics. Each topic includes a traditional quiz, an interactive exercise, and a playable code-simulation game. We anticipate this will take 1 hour or less to complete.

• Complete a pre-survey and a post-survey about your background and experience.

• Automatic Data Collection: The system will automatically collect data about your quiz answers, game submissions, and survey responses. Your name and student ID will be collected to help the research team match your session data with class records, but this information will not be included in any report or publication. Your participation in this study will not affect your class progress in any way.

Risks: The risks are minimal. You may experience mild frustration if you find certain questions or game levels challenging. There are no physical risks involved.

Benefits: You may benefit from practicing C programming skills through an engaging format. However, we cannot guarantee direct benefits from participating.

How will your information be protected?
Your name will not be used in any report. Identifiable research data will be encrypted and password protected. Your responses will be assigned a code number. The list connecting your name to this code will be kept in an encrypted and password protected file accessible only to the research team. When the study is completed and data have been analyzed, the list will be destroyed. Northern Arizona University's Institutional Review Board may review research records for monitoring purposes.

Voluntary Participation and Withdrawal:
Your participation is entirely voluntary. You may choose not to participate or may withdraw at any time without penalty or loss of benefits to which you are otherwise entitled.
